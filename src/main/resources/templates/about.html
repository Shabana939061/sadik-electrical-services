Our Story

Who We Are: Sadik Electrical Services

Founded by Sadik, a certified master electrician with over a decade of hands-on experience, Sadik Electrical Services was established with a singular vision: to deliver dependable, safe, and transparent electrical craftsmanship to the residents and entrepreneurs of Chilakaluripet, Andhra Pradesh.

Electricity powers our daily lives, but faulty wiring, poorly sized circuit breakers, and ungrounded systems pose severe risks of shocks, fire hazards, and appliance failure. We bridge the gap between quality engineering and affordable doorstep services.

Our Standards

Commitment to Quality & Safety

Every job we undertake is guided by rigorous engineering discipline.

Uncompromised Safety

We never cut corners. All wiring installations include proper earth pits, Earth Leakage Circuit Breakers (ELCB/RCCB), and high-temperature flame-retardant conduits.

Certified & Genuine Parts

We install genuine ISI-marked wires (Finolex, Polycab, RR Kabel) and branded modular switches (Anchor, Legrand, GM, Havells) with manufacturer warranties.

Customer Satisfaction

Modern Technology

Equipped with Advanced Diagnostic Tools

Troubleshooting electrical faults requires more than just guesswork. Sadik Electrical Services uses calibrated digital multimeters, insulation megohm testers, phase rotation meters, and non-contact voltage testers to trace hidden faults behind walls without unnecessary damage.

  • Digital multimeters & Clamp meters for precise load measurement
  • Megger insulation testers to catch wire leakage before sparks occur
  • Heavy-duty wall slot chasers for smooth concealed conduit pipe grooves
  • Full safety PPE including 1000V insulated gloves and insulated tools
